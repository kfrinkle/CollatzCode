This is where all the Ticker Tapes are located

ttpXX.bin -- regular ticker tape, no skip evens, for chunksize of 2^XX

ttSEpXX.bin -- regular ticker tape, with skip evens, for chunksize of 2^XX

ttNZpXX.bin -- no zeros ticker tape and displacement array, no skip evens, for chunksize of 2^XX

ttNZSEpXX.bin -- no zeros ticker tape and displacement array, no skip evens, for chunksize of 2^XX
